# Images and type

The pages load these files from this repository. Nothing is fetched from another site at runtime.

## Original artwork

These pictures were generated for Linden Ledger. They are not photographs of a real place or object, and they are not stock. No stock-license credit applies.

| File | Role |
| --- | --- |
| `app/static/images/source/bank-editorial-architecture.jpg` | Source portrait, 864×1152. Ivory travertine arch, curved stair, teal water. |
| `app/static/images/source/bank-dashboard-sculpture-yellow.jpg` | Source of the overview sculpture, 1152×864. Sunflower-yellow, champagne-gold, and frosted-white ribbons on a warm white ground. |
| `app/static/images/login-arch-864.jpg` and `.webp`, plus 640-wide copies | Sign-in photograph. |
| `app/static/images/dashboard-sculpture-1152.jpg` and `.webp`, plus a 768-wide copy | Overview art card. The card behind the image uses the same warm white (`#f2ede9`) so the edges do not show a seam. |
| `app/static/images/source/card-face.jpg` and `card-face-1400.jpg` / `.webp` | Deep blue and gold surface behind the simulated account card. |
| `app/static/images/source/chip-plate.jpg` and `chip-plate-480.jpg` / `.webp` | A blank brushed-gold plate on the account card. It is not a payment-scheme chip. |
| `app/static/images/source/deposit-still.jpg` and `deposit-still-960.jpg` / `.webp` | Deposit page. |
| `app/static/images/source/withdraw-hall.jpg` and `withdraw-hall-960.jpg` / `.webp` | Withdraw page. |
| `app/static/images/source/transfer-arcs.jpg` and `transfer-arcs-960.jpg` / `.webp` | Transfer page. Two original metal arcs, not a network mark. |
| `app/static/images/source/activity-stairs.jpg` and `activity-stairs-1400.jpg` / `.webp` | Activity page. |
| `app/static/images/source/atrium-tall.jpg` and `atrium-tall-800.jpg` / `.webp` | Sign-in, activity, and passphrase pages. |

An earlier navy-ground sculpture remains at `app/static/images/source/bank-dashboard-sculpture.jpg` and is not shown. The cut-out copies made from it have been removed.

The web files are smaller JPEG and WebP derivatives of those sources. Balances, names, and `SIM-` identifiers are HTML text on top of the pictures. They are not printed into the image files.

These pictures are original. They are not logos, wordmarks, holograms, or card art from any payment scheme. Linden Ledger is not affiliated with a card network, and the account card on the overview is a simulated presentation only.

## Type

Newsreader and Source Sans 3, Latin WOFF2 subsets, under the SIL Open Font License 1.1. The files are in `app/static/fonts/`, taken from the Fontsource builds `newsreader@5.2.8` and `source-sans-3@5.2.6`.
